QRGuard Privacy Policy

Effective 24 August 2026

QRGuard is a research-based QR safety aid. It cannot guarantee that a QR code, destination, payment request, or network is safe.

Data processed during a scan

When you request analysis, QRGuard sends the decoded QR payload and, when available, a cropped QR image to the QRGuard analysis server. They are used to calculate structural, semantic, and combined risk results.

The production server does not persist uploaded images or raw payloads by default. It processes them in memory. Operational logs deliberately omit the raw payload and destination domain.

Data stored on your device

If history is enabled, QRGuard stores up to 200 local summaries. A summary may contain a SHA-256 payload hash, registered domain, risk signals, verdict, score, and scan time. It does not contain the raw QR image, full URL, URL path or query, or raw payload. You can disable or clear history.

Optional deep analysis

Deep analysis runs only when requested. If a third-party language-model provider is enabled, relevant link evidence may be sent to it for a second opinion. The initial internal deployment keeps this provider disabled.

Permissions and security

QRGuard requests camera permission for scanning and network access for analysis. Gallery selection is initiated through the operating-system picker. Production traffic uses HTTPS. QRGuard does not sell scan data and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Retention and deletion

Server-side scan inputs are not retained under the default production configuration. Local history remains until it exceeds 200 records, you clear it, or the app is removed. Infrastructure security logs follow the hosting provider's configured retention.
