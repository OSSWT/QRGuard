## Local articles and journals

The PDF files are stored under `articles_and_journals/` and were moved here from
`06_Research_and_Documents/Articles_and_Journals` to remove the second physical
copy/location.

1. Aslam et al., “AntiPhishStack: LSTM-based Stacked Generalization Model for
   Optimized Phishing URL Detection,” 2024,
   [arXiv:2401.08947](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.08947). Local file:
   `2401.08947v2.pdf`.
2. Ovi, Rahman, and Hossain, “PhishGuard: A Multi-Layered Ensemble Model for
   Optimal Phishing Website Detection,” 2024,
   [arXiv:2409.19825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19825). Local file:
   `2409.19825v1.pdf`.
3. Ismail, Ibrahim, and Mahmoud, “A Hybrid, Multi-Layered Pipeline for Phishing
   and Threat Classification: Independently Validated URL and NLP Engines with a
   Calibrated Multi-Channel Fusion Stage,” 2026,
   [arXiv:2606.21690](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21690). Local file:
   `2606.21690v2.pdf`.

   for Phishing Email Detection,” *Electronics* 2026, 15(2), 368,
   [DOI 10.3390/electronics15020368](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics15020368).
   Local file: `electronics-15-00368-v2.pdf`.

The last two papers also inform the shared decision/fusion discussion, but their
learning task is semantic phishing/threat classification, so they are catalogued
here rather than as a third ML branch.
