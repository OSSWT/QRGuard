# Structural Colab notebook

`01_Structural_Training_Colab.ipynb` is a dated convenience snapshot copied from
`QRGuard_ML_Colab/01_Structural_Training_Colab.ipynb`.

Upload the repository's `QRGuard_ML_Colab.zip` to Google Drive and follow the
notebook cells. Expected Drive data layout:

```text
MyDrive/QRGuard_ML_Data/structural/
  QR-DN1.0.zip
  qr_codes_in_surfaces.zip
  runtime_captures/       optional for research; mandatory for deployment approval
```

The notebook verifies the two public archive hashes before training. Raw QR
payloads are not required or stored in runtime-capture metadata.
